Why Capstone Ignite Exists

Millions of women have smartphones and laptops, but lack a structured, safe, and supportive environment to build digital confidence or income. Capstone Ignite was built from scratch as a women-first IT program where every topic connects directly to independence and real-world income.

Designed for Every Woman

The Student (Age 16–25)

Wants IT skills to stand out in university or prepare for future employment.

The Homemaker

Wants to earn online from home without sacrificing household responsibilities.

The Job Seeker

Needs digital confidence (MS Office, Gmail, Canva) expected by modern employers.

Complete 60+ Skills Track

Capstone Pro

A Complete Workplace & Career Breakdown for Students & Parents

11 Modules | 60+ Practical Skills | Real Workplace Tools

11 Modules Breakdown

Module 1 & 2

Computer Fundamentals & Operating Systems

Hardware parts (CPU, RAM, Storage), specs reading, Windows navigation, file compression, software safety, and keyboard shortcuts.

Module 3 & 4

MS Office & Google Workspace

Formatting Word documents, Excel formulas/spreadsheets, PowerPoint slides, Gmail setup, Docs, Sheets, Drive collaboration, and professional email rules.

Module 5 & 6

Internet, Networks & Cybersecurity

LAN/WAN/WiFi, DNS, virus & threat protection, 2FA/OTPs, anti-phishing defense, and data protection — a critical life skill.

Module 7 & 8

Databases & AI Tools for Real Life

Flat vs relational databases, spreadsheet record keeping, prompt writing for ChatGPT & Gemini, homework research, and responsible AI use.

Module 9 & 10

Programming & Web Development

HTML web structure, Scratch logic, Python fundamentals, C/C++ programming basics, building websites with WordPress & Webflow, SEO & live hosting.

Module 11

Creative Design & Freelancing

Canva social graphics, posters, card design, setting up Fiverr & Upwork profiles, proposal writing, pricing services, and client communication.
